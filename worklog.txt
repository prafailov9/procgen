   per paint (fixed with the pre-baked ARGB overlay: paint is now two image blits).

   (broken loop bounds), so everyone sensed targets on one side. Replaced with symmetric

7. [FIXED] Population always collapsed by day 1. Four stacked causes:
   - rabbits could never seek food (food positions carry id=-1, the intent check required an id)
   - rabbits fled TOWARD foxes (flee direction was never inverted)
   - foxes ate at range from a phantom position (feeding used the vision scan and passed x twice)
   - hungry rabbits ignored predators (flee intent was overwritten by the eat/mate scan)
8. [FIXED] Fox population exploded to 11K then collapsed after prey extinction. Added the three
   classic predator-prey stabilizers: per-species reproduction rates (species table), kill-on-catch
   with 50% energy conversion instead of 5/tick nibble-farming, and a satiation gate so full foxes
   stop killing. Plus motive-scaled step chances so fleeing prey can outrun hunting predators.
9. [FIXED] Creatures with no sensed target froze in place: cleared intent byte 0 parsed as a
   valid direction (NORTH) while WANDER fell into an empty switch case. Cleared intents are now
   NO_DIR and Movement's branches are exclusive (directed step OR wander, never both/neither).
10. Age never advances (short[] age sits at start value). Plan: age in DAYS, incremented in
    Metabolism on day rollover (tick % 1440 == 0) — also avoids short overflow.
11. Tuning: verify first sustained coexistence (rabbit waves with fox waves lagging behind).
    Watch HUD for out-of-phase population oscillation.

III. Architecture notes (see Simulation_Architecture.txt and Ecosystem_Rules.txt):
- One owner: sim thread owns the World; UI sees immutable snapshots via AtomicReference.
- Commands flow UI -> sim through the channel; snapshots flow sim -> UI. Two one-way pipes.
- Systems are stateless, run in fixed order, all randomness derives from the world seed with
    per-system stream offsets. Same seed = same history.
- Energy is the only currency: sun -> biomass -> rabbit -> fox. No system creates energy for a
    creature except by taking it from somewhere else.


Coexistence Breakthrough:
SEED: -9070669405368241458
Small World(490x270)
1K creatures total: 900 rabbits, 100 foxes
The codebase state with all tuning knobs: [commit hash: 8fc4908889674af6782664bf27d653a47292dc40; desc: coexistance found]
tuning knob changes:
KNOB                 | Before   | After  | Factor|
-------------------------------------------------|
BASE_GROWTH     	 | 0.05	    |5.50	 |110×   |
UPDATE_TOTAL	     | 7	    |50      |7×     |
biomass flow         | 0.35/tick|275/tick|786×   |
MAX_GROWTH_THRESHOLD | 5.0	    |100.0	 |20×    |
BIOMASS_SPAWN_CHANCE | 0.012	|0.072	 |6×     |

This increased herbivore food source, made it way less scarce.
It allows rabbits to constantly find food.
Single tile biomass has higher quantity, and much lower drain rate than before(instant eat), which
means rabbits stay in the same place for longer. Since growth rate is extremely quick, and rabbit
reproduction chance is already high, rabbits get aggregated at random locations, each within close distance from each other.
These slow moving rabbit clusters allow foxes to locate and ambush rabbits, since they detect prey BEFORE prey detects them.
On the flip side, rabbits are faster. In an endless plain world, rabbits never get caught.
But this world is more realistic: bounded with hard stops (WATER bodies)
Once a chase begins(fox1 HUNTs rabbit1; rabbit1 FLEEs from fox1), if the rabbit
survives the chase, there will always be rabbits sitting idle(eating/mating) in close sight.

This created the highly stable fox population.


Post-breakthrough changes (protecting and generalizing the equilibrium):

1. [FIXED] Biomass growth was an ABSOLUTE rate, not a per-area one.
   BiomassGrowthSystem picked a flat 50 tiles/tick regardless of world size, so every world
   received the same 275 biomass/tick no matter how much land it had:
     - small world 490x270   =   132,300 tiles -> 275/tick
     - big world  1920x1080  = 2,073,600 tiles -> 275/tick, over 15.7x more land
   Initial biomass IS per-tile (BIOMASS_SPAWN_CHANCE), so big worlds started proportionally
   stocked but regrew 15.7x slower per unit area. That is exactly the observed big-world
   signature: rabbits explode on the standing crop (50K), crash when they hit the flow (800),
   and settle at the flow-limited carrying capacity (~5000) which is roughly the SAME absolute
   number the small world supports.
   Foxes then went extinct not from lack of rabbits but from lack of rabbit DENSITY. A fox scans
   (2*10+1)^2 = 441 tiles:
     - small world, 1200 rabbits: ~4.0 rabbits in view -> always a target
     - big world,   5000 rabbits: ~1.1 in view
     - big world during the crash, 800 rabbits: ~0.17 in view -> starvation mid-chase
   Fix: the per-tick growth budget is now worldSize * GROWTH_EVENTS_PER_TILE, calibrated from
   this breakthrough run (50 events / 132,300 tiles). A fractional accumulator carries the
   remainder so worlds too small to earn one whole event per tick (32x32 earns 0.39) do not lose
   it to truncation. Food DENSITY, not food total, is now world-size independent.
   LESSON (carries directly to the economy sim): stock vs flow. Populations boom on accumulated
   stock and starve on renewal rate, and the two look identical for the first few days.

2. [FIXED] growMeadow grew the same tile ~100 times instead of a disc: the inner loop passed the
   center `idx` to growPlant instead of the dx/dy-offset neighbor index. Still commented out of
   the update path, but correct now if re-enabled.

3. [ADDED] EcosystemCoexistenceTest - the tripwire for this equilibrium.
   The coexistence state lives in constants spread across the species table, growth, feeding,
   metabolism and movement. Any of them can be nudged while refactoring and silently return the
   sim to day-1 extinction with nothing else in the build noticing.
     - bothSpeciesSurviveOnTheCoexistenceSeed: runs the recorded scenario (seed
       -9070669405368241458, small world) headless for 12 sim days and asserts both species are
       alive. 12 days covers every collapse mode seen so far (foxes died inside day 1, rabbits
       by day 1-3). Runs the systems directly - no processor timing loop, no threads, no UI.
     - biomassGrowthPerTileIsWorldSizeIndependent: measures growth on creature-free small and big
       worlds and asserts per-tile regrowth matches within 2x. Before fix #1 the ratio was 15.7.
       Creature-free on purpose: measuring net biomass in a populated world measures grazing
       pressure, not growth.
   Whole suite runs in ~16s.

4. [ADDED] StateActor.ofEcosystem(seed) - single source of truth for tick order and per-system
   RNG stream offsets, now used by both AppGuiBootstrapper and the regression test. A test that
   built its own system list would keep passing while production wiring drifted.

5. [ADDED] Observability v2 (partial): population history strip-chart on the HUD.
   Ring buffer sampled once per sim-hour, 336 samples = 14 sim days. Each species is scaled to
   its OWN maximum, because rabbits outnumber foxes ~5:1 and a shared axis flattens the fox line
   into the baseline - hiding exactly the predator dynamics worth watching. Predator-prey
   behaviour is a phase relationship (prey peak -> predator peak later -> prey crash) and a
   single-frame readout cannot show it.
   Also: HUD now counts ALL motives (wander/seek_food/flee/hunt/seek_mate), built by iterating
   the Motive enum so new motives appear automatically.

6. [DOCUMENTED] Water bodies are controlled by elevation, NOT moisture (validated against the
   generator + classifier):
     - elevationFrequency (first NoiseSettings param) = size/count of water bodies. Lower =
       fewer, larger oceans. THIS is the water knob.
     - TerrainClassifier elevation thresholds (<0.21 deep, <0.38 shallow) = total water fraction.
     - Octaves only add coastline/terrain detail, never the number of water bodies.
     - The moisture pair never touches water at all; it only picks GRASS vs SAND/FOREST on land.
   Written into the NoiseSettings javadoc. Note WorldSetupPanel currently passes
   moistureOctaves=8 (default 3) - it has no effect on water.

Open questions / next experiments:
- Is the coexistence driven by geography (river refugia) or purely by prey density? They are
  confounded in the current run. Controlled test: same seed and tuning, world generated with no
  water barriers (single flat continent). If coexistence survives, density was the driver; if
  foxes die, the refugia hypothesis is confirmed as load-bearing.
  Reference: Huffaker 1958 - predator-prey systems go extinct in uniform environments and
  persist for months once spatial barriers are added.
- Now that growth scales with area, re-run the big world: the small-world equilibrium should
  transfer. This is the main untested prediction of fix #1.
- Fox stability (190-300 while rabbits swing 800-1500) traces to FOX.reproductionChance=0.0015
  being ~7x lower than rabbits: fox demography acts as a low-pass filter that damps oscillation.
  Worth keeping.
- Fox:rabbit ratio ~1:5 is high biologically (real systems run 1:100+), traceable to
  PREY_CONVERSION=0.5 vs ~0.1 in nature. Knob to turn if leaner predators are wanted.
