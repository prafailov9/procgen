ECOSYSTEM RULES
General rules for the simulation. Short on purpose - these get shaped as the game grows.

THE BIG PICTURE
Energy is the only currency. Sunlight becomes plant biomass, biomass becomes herbivore energy,
herbivores become predator energy, and death removes energy from the world. No system may create
energy for a creature except by taking it from somewhere else. Growth is the only faucet;
death and living costs are the only drains. If populations explode or collapse, the first
question is always: where is energy leaking in or out?

Every creature follows small local rules: look around, move, eat, pay upkeep, reproduce, die.
Nobody scripts population cycles - they must emerge from these rules or not at all.

TICK ORDER
Systems run in this fixed order every tick. The order tells a tiny story:
perceive, act, eat, pay rent, breed, bury the dead.

  1. Growth        - the world regrows
  2. Behavior      - creatures decide (eyes and brain)
  3. Movement      - creatures act on decisions (legs)
  4. Feeding       - creatures eat what is where they stand (mouth)
  5. Metabolism    - everyone pays the cost of being alive
  6. Reproduction  - surplus energy becomes offspring
  7. Cleanup       - deaths and births actually happen

WHAT EACH SYSTEM IS
Growth: biomass slowly regrows on grass and forest, a few random tiles per tick.
  It never grows on water, sand, hills or mountains, and never past the cap.

Behavior (later): the only system with perception. It scans nearby tiles and writes an
  INTENTION (a chosen direction or target) for other systems to act on. It never moves anyone
  and never changes energy. Until it exists, Movement uses random directions.

Movement: executes steps. Checks the target tile is inside the world and walkable,
  then moves the creature and charges a step cost. It never decides WHY to move.

Feeding: converts one store of energy into another, on the spot.
  Rabbits eat the biomass on their own tile; foxes eat rabbits they stand next to.
  A feeder never gains more energy than the source loses, and never eats past its own maximum.

Metabolism: charges every living creature a small upkeep cost every tick, and advances age
  (age counts DAYS, not ticks). A creature at zero energy or past its lifespan is marked dead.

Reproduction: a creature with a large energy surplus may produce offspring, paying a big
  chunk of its own energy for it. The child spawns nearby, small and hungry. If the store is
  full, no birth happens.

Cleanup: the undertaker. The ONLY system that actually kills or spawns. Everyone else only
  marks. This runs last so no system ever sees a half-dead creature mid-tick.

GENERAL LAWS
1. One owner. Only the sim thread ever touches the world. The UI sees copies.
2. Systems are stateless. All game state lives in the world's stores and layers.
   A system owns nothing but its random generator.
3. Data and logic never mix. Stores hold numbers; systems hold rules.
4. Mark, don't kill. Death and birth during a tick are requests; Cleanup applies them at
   the end. No creature list changes while someone is walking it.
5. Same seed, same history. Every random generator derives from the world seed, each with
   its own twist so no two systems share a stream. A run must be reproducible forever.
6. Tune in sim time, not raw numbers. Constants are chosen from anchors like
   "a rabbit starves in about two days" or "grass regrows in a season" - then converted
   to per-tick values. One tick is one sim minute; one day is 1440 ticks.
7. Everything visible. A system that changes nothing on screen or in the stats does not
   exist. If you cannot see it, you cannot debug it.
8. Predators are rare. Roughly one fox per ten rabbits at spawn, or the food chain
   collapses before it starts.
